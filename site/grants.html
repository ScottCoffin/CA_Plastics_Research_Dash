Each ribbon is a group of awards. Hover or focus a block to follow its flows; click a block to filter every chart to it (click again to clear).

Read amounts with care. Amounts follow each source's own basis (NSF estimated total, NIH fiscal-year total cost, USAspending obligated, and so on). They are not comparable or additive across sources, and some awards have no amount. Ribbon width therefore defaults to the number of awards.

Ribbon width

Awards with several topics split their weight equally across those topics (an award with two topics adds one half to each); awards with no topic go to "Untagged". Within each sector the organisation column shows the largest recipients, and the remainder are grouped as "Other <sector>".
