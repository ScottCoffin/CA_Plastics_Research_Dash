# Policy and regulation

Reference information with outbound links; it makes no statement about implementation, enforcement or effectiveness.

Terms used on this page. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (<abbr title="Department of Toxic Substances Control">DTSC</abbr>) runs the Safer Consumer Products program. The <abbr title="DTSC Safer Consumer Products list of chemicals that may be considered for regulation">Candidate Chemicals list</abbr> is that list of chemicals that may be considered for regulation. A <abbr title="A product–chemical combination that DTSC lists for regulation under Safer Consumer Products">Priority Product</abbr> is a product–chemical combination that DTSC lists for regulation under Safer Consumer Products. The <abbr title="DTSC's Priority Product Work Plan: the three-year list of product categories it will evaluate">Work Plan</abbr> is DTSC's Priority Product Work Plan, the three-year list of product categories it will evaluate. The Ocean Protection Council (<abbr title="Ocean Protection Council">OPC</abbr>) is covered in the Solutions track at the end of this page.
