# Plastiverse Live ambassador contribution terms

Status: approved by the owner as written on 2026-11-25 (H48). Version 1. Ambassadors see this text, in this plain form, on
their first visit and must accept it before contributing.

## What you agree to

1. **You share what you are allowed to share.** You only add information, links, files or data that you have the
   right to share. If you share on behalf of a group or organisation, you confirm that you may do so.
2. **Your contributions are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.** Anything you add to the Atlas (corrections, confirmations,
   descriptions, region proposals, classification answers) is published under the Creative Commons
   Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licence, the same licence as the Atlas data. Monitoring datasets keep their own
   licence, which you state when you share them; nothing is published until that licence is cleared.
3. **Your name is shown with your contributions.** By default, approved contributions say who confirmed them, for
   example "Confirmed by Ingrid Haugland (NIVA)". You can switch this off at any time in Notification settings;
   your contributions then say "Confirmed by a local ambassador". Your Ambassador badge can be hidden separately.
4. **No personal data about other people.** Do not add private contact details, health information or other
   personal data about anyone else. Organisations that publish their own contact route may be listed with it.
5. **Everything is reviewed.** Contributions to the data are checked by the Atlas owner or a region lead before
   they are published, and every published change is kept in the public history of the project.
6. **You can leave at any time.** Ask to close your account and we delete your email address and sign-in data.
   Contributions already published stay in the Atlas under the licence above, credited as you chose; on request we
   change the credit to "a local ambassador".

## What we do with your information

- Your email address is used only to sign you in and to send the emails you can control in Notification settings.
  It is never published, never shared and never added to the project's public files.
- We keep your display name, institution, region, consent date and your contributions.
- Questions or requests: email theplastiverse@gmail.com.

## Changes to these terms

If these terms change, we show the new text the next time you sign in and ask you to accept it again.
