# Image Generation Prompt: Plastiverse Live — From Pollution to Knowledge

## Objective

Create a premium, publication-quality, flat-vector brand illustration and logo for **PLASTIVERSE LIVE**, a scientific data-informatics platform that integrates and synthesizes microplastics research.

The artwork must communicate a visual transformation from **fragmented environmental pollution and scientific information overload** into **structured, interconnected, actionable scientific knowledge**.

The central visual metaphor is **"From Information Deluge to Connected Understanding."**

The logo combines a colorful, organic explosion of microplastic debris on the left with a precise, geometric scientific knowledge network on the right. Both structures connect through a brilliant central convergence point.

The finished image should feel like a sophisticated blend of environmental science illustration, contemporary information design, and playful but meticulously executed vector branding.

## 1. Canvas and Overall Composition

- **Format:** Square, 1:1 aspect ratio.
- **Background:** Pure solid black (#000000).
- **Rendering:** Extremely crisp, high-resolution, 2D flat-vector illustration.
- **Layout:** Two integrated visual systems occupying the upper 75% of the canvas, with a large centered wordmark and website address in the bottom 25%.
- **Margins:** Generous negative space around the composition.
- **Overall impression:** A dynamic, asymmetric illustration in which organic chaos converges into mathematical order.

The composition should resemble two interconnected universes:

**LEFT: A fragmented universe of microplastics.**

Thousands of small, colorful, irregular particles form an organic branching explosion, approximately reminiscent of an abstract starburst, underwater current, or spreading constellation.

**RIGHT: An organized universe of scientific knowledge.**

Circular nodes containing scientific and data-related symbols are connected by fine straight lines into an elegant knowledge graph.

**CENTER: The point of transformation.**

Fine, sweeping, multicolored lines carry the fragmented information toward a luminous yellow central node, symbolizing data synthesis and scientific insight.

The two visual systems should appear naturally integrated rather than disconnected or artificially juxtaposed.

## 2. Left Side: Microplastics and Information Deluge

Occupy approximately 48–52% of the main illustration with hundreds of small, irregularly shaped microplastic fragments.

### Particle appearance

Every fragment must be a distinct flat-colored vector shape.

Use a combination of:

- Angular polygonal shards.
- Small irregular rectangles and quadrilaterals.
- Broken triangular pieces.
- Rounded coral-colored fragments.
- Tiny irregular stars and jagged shapes.
- Elongated slivers.
- Asymmetric pentagons and hexagons.
- Thin, short, irregular flecks.

Particle sizes should vary substantially, from nearly microscopic dots to large, visually prominent fragments.

Avoid uniform particle distributions, repeated decorative patterns, and perfectly regular geometric shapes.

### Color palette

Use these principal colors:

| Color | Approximate hex | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Coral red | #FA6B68 | Dominant debris accent |
| Pale butter yellow | #FFF49A | High-visibility fragments |
| Soft seafoam mint | #C5EADF | Larger fragments and linework |
| Muted slate blue | #436487 | Secondary fragments |
| Dark midnight navy | #17263D | Subtle secondary particles |
| Warm cream | #FFF8DF | Highlights and select details |

All shapes must have solid fills without gradients or shadows.

### Organic distribution

The fragments should collectively create an abstract, radiating shape resembling a large, irregular six-armed starburst.

Distinct branching streams should extend toward:

- The upper-left corner.
- The upper-center area.
- The far-left center.
- The lower-left corner.
- The lower-middle area.
- The central convergence point.

Each branch must have a broken, irregular silhouette.

Particle density should be highest in the center-left region, thinning progressively toward the outer tips.

The overall structure should remain clearly visible against the black background, with substantial black negative space between its arms.

The fragments should evoke a universe of scattered research observations, datasets, publications, and environmental particles.

### Environmental plastic icons

Integrate several larger, recognizable line-art illustrations into the fragmented universe.

These icons should appear embedded within the cloud of microplastic debris, not positioned as separate interface elements.

**A. Rubber duck**

- Located in the upper-left-to-center-left region.
- Simple, friendly, side-view rubber duck.
- Drawn entirely in pale butter-yellow outlines.
- Round head with a small circular eye.
- Curved body and slightly raised tail.
- Minimal internal detailing.
- Approximately 10–12% of the main illustration's width.

**B. Disposable plastic shopping bag**

- Positioned near the upper-middle region of the particle cloud.
- Slightly rotated clockwise.
- Drawn using a coral-red outline.
- Recognizable twin handles and a simple bag silhouette.
- No fill, except for occasional tiny particles visible inside.

**C. Disposable cutlery**

- Located near the upper-center, between the fragmented cloud and central knowledge network.
- Three utensils: spoon, fork, and knife.
- Drawn with thin seafoam-mint outlines.
- Slightly diagonal orientation.
- Clean, minimal silhouettes.

**D. Disposable takeaway cup**

- Positioned toward the lower-left portion.
- Pale mint outline.
- Simple trapezoidal drinking cup, flat lid, and angled straw.
- A few tiny colored fragments visible within the outline.

**E. Plastic water bottle**

- Located in the lower-middle-left region.
- Tilted slightly clockwise.
- Drawn in pale mint outlines.
- Narrow neck and recognizable cap.
- A few subtle internal decorations suggesting plastic fragments.

These icons should be charming, recognizable, and stylistically consistent with the original Plastiverse brand.

## 3. Central Transformation: From Fragmentation to Insight

At approximately 68% of the illustration's width and 45% of its height, position a prominent pale-yellow circular node.

This is the visual and conceptual center of the design.

### Central node

- Large, filled circular disk.
- Pale luminous yellow (#FFF49A).
- Perfectly geometric.
- No internal text or icon.
- Surrounded by approximately 20–26 thin radial yellow strokes, resembling a stylized sunburst.
- The strokes should vary slightly in length while remaining symmetrically distributed.
- Maintain a clean black gap between the disk and the surrounding rays.

The node represents integrated knowledge, synthesis, and orientation.

### Converging evidence streams

From the dense fragment cloud on the left, draw approximately 5–7 elegant, smooth, curved lines flowing into the central yellow node.

Each line should be distinct, thin, and highly controlled.

Use a mixture of:

- Butter yellow.
- Coral.
- Pale mint.
- Muted blue.

The curves should be gently sweeping Bézier arcs, not sharp zigzags.

Their collective shape should resemble a funnel of information transforming chaos into structure.

Most lines should originate near the center-left particle cloud and gradually converge into the central knowledge node.

The visual progression must be unmistakable:

**Scattered particles → flowing streams → consolidated knowledge → interconnected network.**

Do not use arrows, labels, or explanatory text within this transformation.

## 4. Right Side: Scientific Knowledge Network

The right portion of the main illustration should contain an elegant, asymmetric network of circular scientific data nodes.

This organized structure must contrast sharply with the irregular debris field on the left.

### Network architecture

Create a distributed graph with approximately 8–10 primary circular nodes plus several smaller intermediary junctions.

Connect nodes using thin, precisely drawn straight lines.

- Connections should be mostly pale mint or warm cream.
- Some connections may be coral or muted blue.
- Lines should have consistent stroke thickness.
- Nodes should vary moderately in diameter.
- Avoid perfect radial symmetry, rigid grids, or a generic corporate technology pattern.

The network should look scientifically meaningful, somewhat resembling a bibliometric network, knowledge graph, or integrated relational database.

The central yellow convergence node is the primary hub, but several peripheral nodes should also connect to each other.

### Primary scientific nodes

Include the following icons, each enclosed in a thin circular outline with a black interior:

**1. Database**

- Position: Upper-left edge of the knowledge network.
- Yellow outline.
- Stack of three horizontal cylindrical disks.
- Represents structured datasets and repositories.

**2. Layered datasets**

- Position: Near the upper center.
- Mint outline.
- Three stacked rhombus-like layers.
- Represents data integration and interoperable research layers.

**3. Analytics chart**

- Position: Upper-right.
- Coral outline.
- Three ascending vertical bars.
- Represents quantitative analysis and emerging research trends.

**4. Research community**

- Position: Middle-right.
- Muted blue circular outline.
- Three simplified human busts.
- Represents researchers, collaborations, and scholarly networks.

**5. Research institutions**

- Position: Lower-right.
- Pale-yellow outline.
- Classical institutional building with columns and triangular roof.
- Represents universities and scientific organizations.

**6. Geospatial observations**

- Position: Lower-center-right.
- Coral circular outline.
- Familiar map-location pin symbol.
- Represents monitoring sites, geographic information, and spatial data.

**7. Experimental science**

- Position: Bottom-left of the knowledge network.
- Pale-mint outline.
- Laboratory Erlenmeyer flask with small amount of liquid.
- Represents analytical methods and experimental evidence.

**8. Scientific publications**

- Position: Left-lower edge of the network, near the convergence streams.
- Pale-mint outline.
- Document with folded corner and several short text lines.
- Represents published studies and evidence.

### Secondary connection points

Place approximately 5–7 small filled circles at intermediate network junctions.

These should be colored pale yellow, coral, mint, or navy.

Use straight connectors between them and the primary scientific nodes.

The network must feel integrated and deliberately composed, with balanced visual weight.

All pictograms must be exceptionally simple, clean, and readable at reduced sizes.

## 5. Typography and Wordmark

Directly beneath the illustration, place the platform name:

**PLASTIVERSE LIVE**

The wordmark should be centered horizontally and occupy approximately 86–90% of the canvas width.

### Main wordmark treatment

- Uppercase.
- Bold, contemporary geometric sans-serif.
- Similar visual character to Montserrat Bold, Gotham, or Avenir Next Heavy.
- Wide letter spacing, but not excessively spaced.
- Clean, precise letterforms with consistent stroke proportions.
- No texture, distortion, or decorative effects.

Color:

- **PLASTIVERSE:** Pure white (#FFFFFF).
- **LIVE:** Coral red (#FA6B68).

The two words should appear on the same line, with a clear but modest space between them.

The wordmark should be large enough to dominate the lower portion of the composition without overpowering the main graphic.

### Website subtitle

Below the main wordmark, include:

**plastiverse.live**

Style:

- Lowercase.
- Thin geometric sans-serif.
- Very generous letter spacing.
- Pale seafoam mint (#C5EADF).
- Approximately one-third the height of the main wordmark.
- Perfectly centered.

Add a thin horizontal pale-mint decorative line on each side of the website address.

The lines should be symmetrical, approximately 10–13% of the canvas width each.

Maintain generous vertical spacing between the main wordmark and the website address.

## 7. Scientific Meaning and Brand Narrative

The design should embody the conceptual framework introduced by Hartmann, Mennekes, and Wagner (2026), *Lost in a Sea of Information: The Microplastics Publication Deluge*.

The image should communicate three interconnected ideas.

**Fragmentation:** Microplastics research consists of thousands of disparate publications, observations, experimental methods, datasets, and institutions. These are visually represented by the colorful, scattered fragments and pollution iconography.

**Synthesis:** Scientific understanding requires filtering, organizing, connecting, and integrating dispersed information. This process is represented by the curved evidence streams flowing toward the prominent yellow central node.

**Connected knowledge:** Integrated research can reveal relationships between publications, researchers, institutions, geographic locations, analytical measurements, and scientific insights. This is represented by the organized network of interconnected scientific icons.

The design must remain visually compelling without requiring knowledge of these conceptual meanings.

The overall aesthetic should be **optimistic, intellectually sophisticated, modern, and distinctive**, communicating an open scientific infrastructure that transforms the overwhelming quantity of microplastics research into useful, navigable knowledge.

## 8. Final Rendering Instructions

Generate a single exceptionally polished image.

- Square composition.
- Pure black background.
- The full illustration and typography must fit comfortably within the canvas.
- Preserve every icon and word without clipping.
- Maintain meticulous alignment and visual balance.
- Render all shapes with razor-sharp flat-color edges.
- Ensure the scientific network is detailed but not overcrowded.
- Ensure the plastic fragments have natural, irregular distribution.
- Make the transition between the chaotic debris and structured network the dominant visual story.
- Maintain an unmistakable visual connection to the original Plastiverse brand through the palette, microplastic shapes, rubber duck, plastic bag, disposable cutlery, takeaway cup, and water bottle.

**Final result:** A distinctive, premium-quality scientific-informatics brand illustration for PLASTIVERSE LIVE that conveys the transformation from environmental information overload into organized, interconnected scientific understanding.
