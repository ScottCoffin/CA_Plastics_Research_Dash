# Surface brief: Atlas intro page option (Plane Poster)

Scope: standalone intro/landing page option for CA Plastics Research Atlas, for owner comparison against the live Overview. Mode: Persuade. Audiences: researchers, agency staff, funders and policy staff, public and press. Primary action: pick a task (owner answer, 2026-11-16). Constraints: real counts only (16 Nov 2026 snapshot), disclaimer kept, WCAG AA, 375 px.

## Direction contract
THESIS: California is the navigation: the state's silhouette built from four flat planes, one per task, each plane a door. Refuses the hero-search-over-card-grid portal.
OWN-WORLD: paper ground, stone rules, vermilion, indigo, gold, ink. Flat planes only: no outlines, gradients, glass or soft cards. One grotesk at monumental and whisper extremes; tracked caps for metadata only. Active controls get a diagonal ink corner.
STORY: The visitor sees California as four colours, reads one line, picks a door. Below, each door repeats as a band with live counts and their source, then how the Atlas stays current, then search and the disclaimer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: left half: headline about 88px, one lead line, four door rows (swatch, task, count) as the primary action, search below. Right half: the four-plane California figure at full height; hovering or focusing a row lifts its plane and stills the others.
FORM: challenger tanaka-plane-portrait (competitive card, owner-chosen); seed:d6d6c0290ca009e1. Signature interaction: row and plane highlight each other.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
