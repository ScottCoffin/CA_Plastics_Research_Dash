Become a local ambassador

Plastiverse Live grows region by region. Researchers and community leaders who know a place check our data there and add what only locals know.

What ambassadors do

About an hour a month, from your own desk.

  • Check doubtful matches

    The automatic sorting is unsure about some works and institutions. You say whether it is right: a place name in the wrong country, or a Norwegian-language study that is about plastics.

  • Confirm placeholders

    Some funders, monitoring programmes and policies on a region page are not yet checked. Confirm the name and link, correct them, or mark them not relevant.

  • Add local knowledge

    Tell us about community groups, volunteer clean-ups, local policies with their dates, and monitoring data with its licence.

Can I join? Check your institution

Type your work email or your institution's name. We check it against the institutions already tagged for plastics research in the region.

Free email services (Gmail, Outlook, Proton and similar) cannot be accepted.

How it works

Three steps. No passwords.
  1. Sign in with your institution email. We send a one-time link that works for 30 minutes.
  2. Set your display name once and agree to the contribution terms (CC BY-NC 4.0, the same licence as the Atlas data).
  3. Work on your region's three streams. Nothing you add reaches the Atlas until a reviewer approves it.
