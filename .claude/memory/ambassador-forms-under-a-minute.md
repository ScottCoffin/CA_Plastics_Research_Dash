Ambassador workspace forms (e.g. Tag local groups) should let a volunteer finish one item in under a minute. Never make users type or understand codes (ISO 3166 places, ISO 639 languages): offer pickers by plain name, prefill what the system already knows (group URL, country, region language, today's date), use one shared evidence quote rather than one fieldset per field, keep optional parts collapsed, and auto-advance to the next item. Every action button whose meaning is not obvious (e.g. "Renew lease", "Return to the pool") gets an accessible tooltip. Keep separate tasks (e.g. suggesting a missing group) on their own screen, not on a single item's page.

**Why:** owner feedback 2026-11-26 on the CG-6c group-tagging form (too long, ISO-code inputs, duplicate evidence sections, suggest form mixed in).

**How to apply:** check any new or changed ambassador form against these rules before handing it off; related: [[delegate-to-haiku-first]], [[review-apps-over-10-cases]].
